Our Love Story

Sarah and Michael first met in college during a literature class. Michael was immediately drawn to Sarah's passion for poetry, while Sarah admired Michael's thoughtful perspectives.

Their first date was at a small coffee shop where they talked for hours, discovering their shared love for travel, old movies, and Italian food. That coffee date turned into many more, and soon they were inseparable.

On a trip to Paris, Michael proposed at sunset by the Seine River, presenting Sarah with a beautiful vintage ring that had belonged to his grandmother. Of course, she said yes!

Now, after five wonderful years together, they're excited to begin this new chapter as husband and wife, surrounded by all the people they love.
